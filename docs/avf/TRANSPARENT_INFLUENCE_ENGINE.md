# Transparent Influence Engine

## Role

The Transparent Influence Engine creates approval-gated content and distribution plans for owned channels without fake human impersonation, bot networks, spam, astroturfing, brigading, engagement manipulation, or platform bypass.

## Allowed Direction

- transparent AI brand or AI creator positioning
- owned-channel content planning
- blog drafts
- newsletter drafts
- official SNS drafts
- community post drafts
- comment and reply drafts
- image prompt drafts
- video prompt drafts
- human approval before publishing
- performance evidence after approved publishing in a future phase

## Pipeline

```text
Market Signal
-> Audience Insight
-> Content Thesis
-> Channel Strategy
-> Content Draft
-> Media Prompt
-> Human Approval
-> Publish in a future authorized phase
-> Performance Evidence
-> Strategy Revision
```

## Prohibited Actions

- fake human impersonation: blocked
- undisclosed bot networks: blocked
- spam: blocked
- mass posting without approval: blocked
- engagement manipulation: blocked
- astroturfing: blocked
- brigading: blocked
- personal account automation: blocked
- platform bypass: blocked
