# Strategy & Hypothesis Engine

## Role

The Strategy & Hypothesis Engine turns market signals into testable venture hypotheses.

It must answer two questions for every strategy:

1. If this is right, what evidence should appear soon?
2. If this is wrong, what failure signal should appear first?

## Outputs

- market thesis
- target segment
- wedge strategy
- product hypothesis
- content hypothesis
- brand hypothesis
- distribution hypothesis
- monetization hypothesis
- failure scenario
- experiment plan

## Decision Contract

Every hypothesis must produce:

- success criteria
- failure criteria
- evidence source
- time box
- next decision: keep, refine, pivot, or kill
