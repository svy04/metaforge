## Findings

1. Mimesis Engineering should be defined as structure extraction plus evidence gating, not style copying.
2. The strongest marketable phrase is proof-bounded: "source-first improvement loop" and "standards, not roles."
3. Open-source systems provide reusable grammar for agent runtime, handoffs, durable execution, and policy gates, but not immediate production claims.
4. Papers provide task-loop patterns: reason-act, reflect, debate, retrieve, evaluate.
5. Patents and standards are useful as prior-art and control vocabulary, not as permission or compliance evidence.
6. Digital Factory currently contains the freshest local Mimesis method and should supersede older profile links or inaccessible public claims.

## Decision Log Impact

- Add a decision that Mimesis Engineering is the Metaforge improvement engine.
- Treat profile README refresh as the next visible proof slice.
- Keep the claim boundary explicit: no production readiness, external validation, compliance, or universal-improvement claim.
